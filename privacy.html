Privacy Policy

DigitalMitra Business · Effective October 4, 2026

DigitalMitra Business is a cloud business-management application for small businesses. This policy explains what information the service handles and how it is used.

Information we collect

Depending on how the service is used, we may store account information such as login email, business/shop name, customer names, mobile numbers, products, inventory, sales, services, payment records, and account status or expiry information.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate users, provide business-management features, synchronize data across devices, calculate sales and customer balances, generate invoices, and provide account support.

WhatsApp sharing

If you choose to share an invoice or service receipt through WhatsApp, DigitalMitra prepares a message containing the information you selected. Sending the message is performed through WhatsApp or your device; DigitalMitra does not control WhatsApp's handling of that message.

Data security

Business data is stored in user-isolated cloud database records. Access controls are used so customers can access only their own business data. We do not ask users to provide passwords to support staff.

Payments

Paid plans may use third-party payment services. Payment processing is subject to the payment provider's terms and privacy policy. DigitalMitra stores limited order/account information needed to associate a payment with a DigitalMitra account.

Data retention and deletion

Account and business data may be retained while an account is active and as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or maintain security. For account/data deletion requests, contact us using the support details below.

Children

DigitalMitra Business is intended for business users and is not designed for children.

Changes

We may update this policy when the service changes. The effective date at the top will be updated when material changes are made.
