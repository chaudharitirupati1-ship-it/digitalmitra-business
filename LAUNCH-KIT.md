# DigitalMitra Business — Launch Kit

## Product
DigitalMitra Business is a simple cloud business-management app for Indian small businesses.

### Core features
- Product and inventory management
- New sales and sales history
- Profit tracking
- Customer records and outstanding dues
- Service-job management
- WhatsApp invoice sharing
- Cloud sync across devices
- Admin-created customer accounts
