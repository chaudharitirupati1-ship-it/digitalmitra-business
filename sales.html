Frequently asked questions

Will my data remain after I change or delete the app?

Your business records are stored in the cloud and linked to your account, so reinstalling the app does not by itself erase them.

Do I get my own login?

Yes. DigitalMitra creates an individual account for each purchasing business.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The interface is designed for mobile use.

Can I manage customer credit?

Yes. You can record credit sales and manage customer outstanding dues.
